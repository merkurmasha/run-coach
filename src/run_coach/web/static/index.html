Timezone & units

How am I progressing?

Weekly distance ?Sum of running distance per ISO calendar week (Monday–Sunday) within the selected range. Marked points are activities you've tagged as a race.
Pace trend ?Weekly average pace (total distance ÷ total moving time that week), plus a trailing 4-week rolling average to smooth out single noisy weeks.
Aerobic efficiency (pace at reference heart rate) ?Each week, a least-squares line is fit to pace vs. average heart rate across the trailing 8 weeks of runs (minimum 8 qualifying runs), then read at your reference heart rate. A falling line means more speed for the same effort.
Personal-bests timeline ?Every time a whole-activity 5K/10K/half-marathon best was improved, across your entire history (not limited by the range switcher above).
Race time estimates (all-time personal bests) ?Riegel's formula: T2 = T1 × (D2/D1)^1.06, extrapolated from your longest all-time personal best. Always an estimate, not a guarantee.
