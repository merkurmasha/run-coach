Timezone & units

Progress

Monthly distance ?Sum of distance per calendar month for every activity whose type contains "run", Garmin and Strava combined after deduplication.
Race time estimates ?Riegel's formula: T2 = T1 × (D2/D1)^1.06, extrapolated from your longest personal best. Always an estimate, not a guarantee.

Aerobic efficiency

Monthly pace at reference heart rate ?Each month, a least-squares line is fit to pace vs. average heart rate across that month's runs (minimum 8 runs), then read at your reference heart rate. A falling line means more speed for the same effort.

Load & distribution

Weekly volume (km) ?Sum of running distance per ISO calendar week (Monday–Sunday).
Time in heart-rate zone ?Share of total zone-tagged minutes across all running activities where Garmin provided per-zone time. Activities without zone data aren't counted in the denominator.

Recovery ?Your most recent 7 days compared to your own trailing 28-day baseline (the 28 days before that week). Garmin data only.
